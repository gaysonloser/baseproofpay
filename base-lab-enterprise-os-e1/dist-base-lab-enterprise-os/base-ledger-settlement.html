Base Ledger Settlement Controls

A read-only operating surface for mapping Base Ledger settlement stages into the enterprise evidence rail. It preserves the separation between private onchain settlement, canonical business events, and human-reviewed ERP drafts.

Design verified · No Base Ledger session or payment executed

1. Deposit

Base fact

Portal deposit establishes an onchain asset entry point.

Enterprise rule

Capture intent, asset, amount and business reference before draft handoff. A deposit notification alone cannot post accounting.

2. Hold & transact

Base fact

Transfers remain private within the ledger.

Enterprise rule

Publish only a canonical event hash and review state. Counterparty payment detail remains outside the public evidence page.

3. Withdraw

Base fact

Withdrawal exposes final asset and amount on Base.

Enterprise rule

Receipt, finality, idempotency and human review are all required before any draft-only ERP handoff.

Boundaries

  • No wallet connection, signature, deposit, transfer or withdrawal has been requested.
  • No ERP credential is present and this console cannot create, submit or post ERP records.
  • A real settlement must have a separately approved payer, asset, amount, receipt/finality readback and ERP draft-only scope.

This page is an implementation-ready control map, not evidence that a Base Ledger settlement has happened.
